The idea in one paragraph

Client and provider agree, milestone by milestone, what evidence counts as “done”. The client's money for each tranche is held in the client's PayPal account. The provider submits that evidence; rule-based checks and an AI verifier comment on it; the client approves with a signed decision; PayPal captures that milestone's amount to the provider. If they disagree, only that milestone is frozen and a three-tier process (sealed offers, evidence vault, mediator) decides the split.
