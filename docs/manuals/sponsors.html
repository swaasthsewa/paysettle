Signing in

Open the live demo, choose Try the live demo (or go to /login) and pick the person named below under "Explore the live demo". No password is needed in the sandbox. To change person, use Switch person in the menu.

AG Grid: one minute

Package: AG Grid Community (ag-grid-community, ag-grid-react). Where: every console table, on 12 screens, through one shared wrapper with the app's own theme (src/components/console/DataTable.tsx, src/components/grid/).

  1. Sign in as Farah Ali (Finance) and open Reconciliation (/finance/recon): each tranche's contract, ledger and PayPal planes side by side; then Ledger (/finance/ledger): the invariant per tranche.
  2. In any grid: click a column header to sort, type in the search box to filter, use Columns to choose columns, and Export CSV to download what you see.
  3. Switch to Olivia Grant (Operations) and open Overview (/ops): contract clearance, holds near PayPal's window and escalated reviews, each a grid with its own SLA and risk columns.

Bryntum: one minute

Package: Bryntum Gantt (the sponsor trial, @bryntum/gantt-trial with @bryntum/gantt-react), which also provides the Scheduler. Where: the contract timeline and the mediators' schedule, read-only (GanttView.tsx, SchedulerView.tsx).

  1. Sign in as Priya Shah (client), open Contracts → Travel booking web app → Timeline. The Signed plan tab shows every milestone on a calendar with where its money is (held in PayPal, released, gate), each tranche's PayPal hold window and today.
  2. Open the Proposed change order tab: the proposed plan drawn over the signed plan as baselines, with re-dated and added milestones marked. Nothing changes until both parties sign.
  3. Open Contract chain (2 periods): period 1 and its continuation into period 2 on one calendar.
  4. Switch to Marcus Bell (mediator) and open Schedule (/mediator/schedule): the Bryntum Scheduler shows every open case per mediator against its deadline (sealed offers, evidence vault, verdict).

Render

The live demo runs on Render as a Docker web service with a Postgres database, defined as a Blueprint (render.yaml) with a health check (/api/health) and automatic deploys.
