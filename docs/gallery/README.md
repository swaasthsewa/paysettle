# PaySettle gallery

Images of PaySettle in four sets: explainer slides, screens from the live app, frames from the video, and the
architecture. Select an image to open it at full size. The role manuals have their own screenshots in
[`docs/screens/`](../screens); this gallery is separate from them.

- [Explainer slides](#explainer-slides) (16)
- [Live app](#live-app) (12)
- [From the video](#from-the-video) (10)
- [Architecture](#architecture) (2)

Everything shown runs on PayPal Sandbox; no real money moves. The live-app screens were captured on
[paysettle.onrender.com](https://paysettle.onrender.com) on 12 Nov 2026 with the demo people; a sandbox payout address
and a network hint are blurred.
